The marketplace gets clearer: every paid plugin says who makes it and who sells it, and installing plugins is more robust on Windows.

## What is new

- **Who makes it and who sells it**: a paid plugin's page now reads "Made by [author]. Sold by Lemon Squeezy (Merchant of Record)". Cuelith sells nothing and never sees your payment details.
- **No commission**: the project charges authors no percentage and the purchase text no longer mentions referral references. The price is the author's store price.
- **"Canti" becomes "Brani" in Italian**: the plugin and the interface texts use a more general name, suitable for any kind of event. It works as before. (In English it was already "Songs".)
- **More robust plugin installation on Windows**: if an antivirus or another program keeps the folder busy for a moment, Cuelith retries instead of showing an error.

## Good to know

- Italian **0.2.3** and English **0.1.3** languages are included in the installer. The **Brani 0.5.2** plugin installs from the marketplace.
- Plugins you already installed and your files do not change.
