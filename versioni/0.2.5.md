Cuelith has a new licence: from this version the program is free software under the GPL 3.0.

## What is new

- **GPL 3.0 or later licence**: the program stays free forever. Anyone who distributes it, modified or not, must provide the source code under the same licence.
- **Plugins stay free to choose their own licence**, including paid and closed-source ones, thanks to a written exception (`PLUGIN-EXCEPTION.md`).
- The licence text and the exception are included in the installer, and Settings → About shows "GPL 3.0".
- **Songs 0.5.1** plugin and **Italian 0.2.1** and **English 0.1.1** languages: the same features as before, under the new licence.

## Good to know

- The program works like 0.2.0: nothing changes in how you use it, in your files or in the plugins you have installed.
- Earlier versions, up to 0.2.0, were published under the Apache 2.0 licence.
